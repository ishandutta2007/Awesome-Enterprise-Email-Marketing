# Awesome-Enterprise-Email-Marketing

## Top Enterprise Email Marketing Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Campaign Automation, Deliverability & Audience Segmentation*  

**Last updated: March 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Enterprise Email Marketing**. These tools enable organizations to design, send, and analyze email campaigns at scale — with automation workflows, subscriber segmentation, A/B testing, and deliverability management.



**Examples** include Salesforce Email Studio, Marketo Engage, Oracle Eloqua, Acoustic Campaign, Mailchimp Enterprise, Campaign Monitor, Constant Contact, Klaviyo, Braze, and Iterable (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom automation workflows, and open email infrastructure — ideal for marketing teams, developers, and organizations seeking transparent, data-sovereign email marketing.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Salesforce Email Studio](https://www.salesforce.com/)**  

  Enterprise email marketing within Marketing Cloud. Features Email Studio, Journey Builder, Content Builder, and Einstein AI for send-time optimization and predictive scoring.



- **[Marketo Engage (Adobe)](https://business.adobe.com/products/marketo/marketo-engage.html)**  

  Enterprise B2B marketing automation with sophisticated email campaign management, lead nurturing, and revenue attribution.



- **[Oracle Eloqua](https://www.oracle.com/)**  

  Enterprise B2B marketing automation platform with email marketing, lead scoring, and campaign orchestration.



- **[Acoustic Campaign](https://acoustic.com/)**  

  Enterprise email marketing and automation platform with AI-powered content recommendations and deliverability tools.



- **[Mailchimp Enterprise](https://mailchimp.com/)**  

  Enterprise email marketing with advanced segmentation, automation, and integrations. Includes transactional email and marketing CRM.



- **[Campaign Monitor](https://www.campaignmonitor.com/)**  

  Email marketing platform known for beautiful templates and drag-and-drop builder. Includes automation and segmentation.



- **[Constant Contact](https://www.constantcontact.com/)**  

  Email marketing platform for small businesses with automation, templates, and list management.



- **[Klaviyo](https://www.klaviyo.com/)**  

  E-commerce email marketing and SMS platform with deep Shopify integration, segmentation, and revenue attribution.



- **[Braze](https://www.braze.com/)**  

  Cross-channel customer engagement platform with sophisticated email marketing, Canvas journey builder, and real-time personalization.



- **[Iterable](https://iterable.com/)**  

  Cross-channel growth marketing platform with email, push, SMS, and in-app messaging. Features visual journey builder and real-time segmentation.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Mautic](https://github.com/mautic/mautic)**  

  The world's largest open-source marketing automation platform (GPL-3.0). 100% community-owned with no commercial entity behind it. Features multi-channel campaigns (email, SMS, social, web tracking), intelligent automation, unlimited segmentation, A/B testing, landing pages, lead scoring, and full data sovereignty. Used by 40,000+ companies including DB bahn.business. Self-hosted with unlimited contacts, no license costs, no vendor lock-in. Supports GDPR compliance .



- **[Notifuse](https://github.com/Notifuse/notifuse)**  

  Modern self-hosted email marketing and transactional email platform (license varies). Built with Go and React. Features visual MJML drag-and-drop editor, Liquid templating, visual flow builder for multi-step automations, 7 sending providers (Amazon SES, Mailgun, Postmark, SendGrid, SparkPost, Mailjet, SMTP), A/B testing, subscriber segmentation, and privacy-first cookieless web analytics (Staminads). Self-hosted with no per-email pricing. Cloud option from $16/month .



- **[Listmonk](https://github.com/knadh/listmonk)**  

  High-performance, self-hosted newsletter and mailing list manager (AGPL-3.0) with a modern dashboard. Built in Go with Docker support. Features subscriber management, campaign creation, templates, and bounce processing. Lightweight and fast alternative to Mailchimp for newsletters and announcements .



- **[Keila](https://github.com/pentacent/keila)**  

  Reliable and easy-to-use newsletter tool (AGPL-3.0) positioned as an alternative to Mailchimp and Sendinblue. Built with Elixir/Phoenix. Features opt-in subscriber management with double opt-in confirmation, drag-and-drop campaign editor plus raw HTML mode, hCaptcha/Friendly Captcha support, automatic bounce/complaint handling, click and open tracking, and per-sender SMTP configuration. Uses Postgres-backed Oban for background jobs — no separate Redis dependency .



- **[Helio](https://github.com/achref-soua/helio)**  

  Open-source, AI-native growth platform (AGPL-3.0) unifying CDP, segmentation, and cross-channel journeys (email/SMS/push/WhatsApp) on Temporal workflow engine. Features natural-language segments, predictive scoring, brand-voice content generation, and MCP server for AI agents. Self-hostable via Docker, Helm, or cloud-init. Sustains ~20,000 events/s at p95 81ms .



- **[Reloop](https://github.com/reloop-labs/reloop)**  

  Open-source transactional email API and self-hostable Resend alternative (Apache-2.0 with use restrictions). Features REST API and SMTP relay for transactional sending, broadcast and sequence campaigns with segmentation, inbound email parsing, visual drag-and-drop editor, real-time analytics in PostgreSQL, webhooks, contacts and lists management, and workflows for multi-step automation. Self-host on Docker, Kubernetes, or bare metal .



- **[Posta](https://hub.docker.com/r/jkaninda/posta)**  

  Self-hosted email delivery and inbound platform (license varies) for developers and teams. Features REST API for transactional and batch emails, built-in SMTP receiver, email relay, versioned multi-language templates, bulk campaigns with A/B testing, domain verification (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), API keys with IP allowlisting, OAuth/SSO login, and Prometheus integration. Designed as fully self-hostable alternative to SendGrid, Mailgun, and Postmark .



- **[phpList](https://github.com/phpList/phplist3)**  

  Newsletter and email marketing platform (AGPL-3.0) with advanced subscriber management, bounce handling, and plugin architecture. One of the longest-running open-source email marketing tools .



- **[Ermes](https://github.com/giaggiola/Ermes)**  

  Self-hosted, open-source email marketing and automation platform (AGPL-3.0) specifically for Shopify. Features visual flow builder with drafts and versioned publishing, campaign management and scheduling, subscribers/segments/suppressions, Resend sending with signed delivery-event webhooks, consent records and preferences, and guided Shopify connection with order/customer/product syncing. Current preview supports one store per installation .



- **[Kling.to](https://github.com/Kling-to/kling)**  

  Self-hosted marketing automation for e-commerce (BSL 1.1, converts to Apache 2.0 after 4 years). Features AI-powered campaign creation (describe intent, Kling builds it), multi-channel sending (email, SMS, WhatsApp, RCS, push), visual automation flows for cart abandonment/welcome series/win-back campaigns, 30+ segmentation filters including predicted LTV and churn risk, and integrations with Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Wix, Magento, and Salesforce Commerce. No per-contact pricing .



- **[CastMail2List](https://pypi.org/project/castmail2list/)**  

  Lightweight, self-hosted mailing list application (license varies). Polls standard IMAP mailboxes for incoming messages, distributes them to subscribers, and provides a web interface for list management. Features broadcast mode (one-to-many), group mode (many-to-many discussion), sender authorization, automatic bounce detection, duplicate prevention, and REST API. No MTA configuration required — works with any email provider offering IMAP and SMTP .



- **[Mailat](https://github.com/dublyo/mailat)**  

  Self-hosted email platform (license varies) for transactional emails, unified inbox, and marketing campaigns using AWS SES. Features REST API for single and batch sending, templates, webhooks with outgoing and incoming (SES/SNS) support, real-time SSE updates, unified inbox across identities, labels, and API key management. Go backend with GoFrame .

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Mautic** as the core marketing automation engine with **Notifuse** or **Keila** for newsletter delivery, **Listmonk** for high-performance bulk sending, **Helio** for AI-native segmentation and cross-channel journeys, and **PostgreSQL** for persistence — creating a fully self-hosted, enterprise-grade email marketing platform.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Email marketing platforms handling subscriber data must comply with data protection regulations (GDPR, CAN-SPAM, CCPA, etc.).

- Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper security hardening, email deliverability configuration (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), and ongoing maintenance for production reliability. Deliverability depends heavily on IP reputation and sender authentication.
